01 About

Building Stronger Academic Systems Through People, Research & Data.

I'm a Master of Commerce graduate with hands-on experience in academic administration, institutional quality assurance and research support within a university environment.

As Academic Assistant at CMS, JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), my work spans NAAC/IQAC accreditation support, ERP-based academic operations, Teaching-Learning-Evaluation Plan (TLEP) coordination, Board of Studies (BoS) activities, research proposal and publication review, and institutional event and training coordination.

I bring IBM SPSS and advanced Microsoft Excel to data analysis and reporting, along with prior experience in HR administration, enterprise technical support and a higher-education teaching practicum. I'm the author of four research papers spanning consumer behaviour, sustainability and governance — and I'm keen to contribute to academic operations and finance-oriented institutional decision-making.
